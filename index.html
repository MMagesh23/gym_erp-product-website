Members by plan

One member, start to renewal.

The same record follows a member through every step, so nobody retypes a phone number or hunts for an old receipt.

  1. 1

    Join

    Add the member with a photo, contact details, emergency contact and health notes. They get an ID such as GYM0001.

  2. 2

    Pick a plan

    Assign a membership plan. Change the plan, freeze it or transfer it later without starting over.

  3. 3

    Take payment

    Record the payment and generate a PDF invoice with the amount in words. Refunds are tracked too.

  4. 4

    Send a reminder

    The dashboard lists memberships about to expire. Send the WhatsApp or email reminder from your own template.

  5. 5

    Renew

    The member renews, and revenue, profit and the day's cash closing update with it.
